London Fog Cookies

Cookies

  • 12 Tbs unsalted butter
  • 3 Earl Grey teabags, cut open (or 1½ Tbs loose-leaf tea, coarsely ground)
  • ½ tsp culinary lavender, optional, plus more for decorating
  • ½ cup plus 2 Tbs/116 grams granulated sugar
  • ½ packed cup/100 grams light brown sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, such as Diamond Crystal
  • 1 ½ cups/206 grams all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 large egg

Steps

  • Heat the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Once it is mostly melted, add the tea and stir for a minute. Add the lavender, if using, then remove the pot from heat. Steep, covered, for 10 minutes.
  • In a mixing bowl, combine the granulated sugar, brown sugar, vanilla and salt. To a small bowl, add the flour, baking powder and baking soda and whisk to combine.
  • Without straining, pour the tea-infused butter over the sugar. Using a silicone spatula, vigorously stir to combine. Make sure the batter is not sizzling hot, then add the egg and vigorously stir for 1 to 2 minutes to combine while cooling down the batter. Add the dry ingredients and mix until there are no dry flour spots. Chill the cookie dough uncovered for 10 minutes in the fridge.
  • Divide the dough into eight ¼-cup balls (80 grams) and place on a small parchment-lined sheet pan or a large plate and freeze for 45 minutes, uncovered, until firm to the touch.
  • Arrange racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven. Heat the oven to 350 degrees. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper. Arrange frozen cookie dough on the sheets, leaving 5 inches between each cookie.
  • Bake for about 16 minutes, until the cookies look lightly golden and puffed up, while the centers still look a bit underbaked. Firmly knock the bottom of the pans against a hard heat-proof surface a few times to flatten the cookies. Cool for 5 minutes, transfer to a cooling rack to cool completely.

Glaze

  • 3 Tbs unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp whole milk
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Salt

Steps

  • Microwave the butter for about 30 seconds, until very soft but not completely melted.
  • Add the sugar, milk, vanilla and a tiny pinch of salt. Whisk until smooth.
  • Drizzle one or two thick stripes of frosting on each room-temperature cookie using a silicone spatula. Decorate with more lavender if you like.
