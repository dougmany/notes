Pea Salad

Dressing

  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 1 Tbsp. mayonnaise
  • 1 Tbsp. white vinegar
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  • In a small bowl, mix ingredients together.

Salad

  • 4 cups frozen green peas, almost totally thawed
  • 8 slices bacon, cooked until crisp and chopped
  • 1/2 small red onion, halved and sliced very thin
  • 6 oz. cheddar or American cheese, cut into small cubes
  • 3 Tbsp. minced fresh parsley, plus more for serving

Steps

  • Stir two-thirds of the dressing into the peas until the peas are coated.
  • Gently stir in the bacon, onion, cheese, and parsley until all combined.
  • Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate 2 to 4 hours before serving.
  • Remove from the fridge and stir in the rest of the dressing to your liking.
  • Sprinkle with more parsley before serving.
